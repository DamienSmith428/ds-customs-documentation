GS Dispensary Job

Full-featured cannabis dispensary job — grow, harvest, roll, stock, sell, and manage your crew — by GunSmoke Creations

QBCore FiveM Lua 5.4 qs-inventory / ox_inventory / qb-inventory oxmysql Escrow

1 — Overview

The GS Dispensary Job is a complete, player-run cannabis business. It ships as two separate resources that are functionally identical — each themed for a different location on your server:

HotBox Dispensary

  • Resource: gs-hotboxdispensary-job
  • Job name: hotbox
  • DB tables prefix: hotbox_
  • Receipt item: hotbox_receipt

White Widow Weed Shop

  • Resource: gs-whitewidow-job
  • Job name: whitewidow
  • DB tables prefix: whitewidow_
  • Receipt item: ww_receipt
Both resources share the same config structure, events, callbacks, and gameplay loop. This page documents both — differences between the two are called out inline where they exist.

Feature summary

  • Plant, grow, and harvest 6 cannabis strains at configurable world spots
  • Roll harvested weed into joints at the rolling station (requires rolling paper)
  • Employee-stocked store with live DB inventory and owner-adjustable prices
  • Customer shop UI — browse by category, pay with cash
  • Employee billing terminal — charge customers directly from a cash register target zone
  • Full HR menu — hire, fire, promote, demote, view roster with employee mugshots
  • Business bank account integration via gs-businessbanking
  • Dispensary effects dependency (gs-dispensaryeffects-dependency)
  • Purchase receipts stored in DB — useable item opens a custom NUI receipt
  • Employee mugshots via screenshot-basic + fivemanage.com
  • Admin in-game plant-spot setup via /hotboxsetupplants or /wwsetupplants
  • Supports qs-inventory, ox_inventory, and qb — auto-detected at startup

3 — Installation

  1. Place the resource folder inside your resources directory.
  2. Import the SQL files (see §4 below).
  3. Add the job entry from SQL + items + job/job.txt to your qb-core/shared/jobs.lua.
  4. Add all item entries from SQL + items + job/items.txt to your inventory shared items file.
  5. Add ensure <resource-name> to server.cfg after all dependencies.
  6. Configure config.lua (API key, positions, strains — see §5).
  7. Set up plant spots in-game using the admin command (see §8).
# server.cfg — ensure dependencies before the job resource
ensure gs-dispensaryeffects-dependency
ensure ds_notify
ensure gs-businessbanking

# Then ensure the job resource(s) you are using
ensure gs-hotboxdispensary-job
ensure gs-whitewidow-job
Both resources can run simultaneously on the same server — they use entirely separate DB table prefixes, job names, and event namespaces.

The growth value is in minutes. A value of 1 means plants are ready to harvest after 1 minute. Increase this for a more realistic grow cycle.

Receipt Settings

Config.ReceiptItem    = "hotbox_receipt"  -- item name in DB  (ww_receipt for White Widow)
Config.ReceiptEnabled = true              -- set false to disable receipt items entirely

Store Allowed Items

Only items listed in Config.Store.AllowedItems can be deposited to or sold from the store. Add or remove entries here to control the store catalogue. The price is the default — it can be changed in-game by rank 3+ employees.

Config.Store.AllowedItems = {
    ["rolling_paper"]    = { label = "Rolling Papers",    price = 10,  category = "Misc" },
    ["whitewidow_joint"] = { label = "White Widow Joint", price = 200, category = "Joints" },
    ["weed_whitewidow"]  = { label = "White Widow Weed",  price = 200, category = "Bud" },
    -- ... (6 strains as Bud + 6 as Joints + Misc)
}

7 — Gameplay Loop

Going On / Off Duty

Walk to Config.Locations.Duty and press E. The 3D prompt turns green when off duty and red when on duty. The server validates job name before toggling.

Planting

  1. Collect a seed from Config.Locations.seedPickup — press E to open the strain picker.
  2. Walk to an empty plant spot (props registered by an admin — see §8) and press E.
  3. Select a strain. The resource checks your inventory for the matching seed, removes it, plays the planting animation, and notifies the server.
  4. The plant is saved to the DB. A growth timer runs server-side — plants become harvestable after growth minutes (configurable per strain).

Harvesting

  1. Collect an empty_weed_bag from Config.Locations.bagCollection.
  2. Walk to a grown plant (prompt reads "[E] Harvest") and press E.
  3. The server removes your empty bag, gives you the harvested weed item, and clears the plant from the DB.

Rolling Joints

Walk to Config.Locations.rollStation and press E. The server checks for any weed item and a rolling_paper in your inventory. If both are present, a QBCore progress bar plays the rolling animation, then the weed and paper are swapped for the corresponding joint item.

Stocking the Store

Walk to Config.Locations.employeeStore. The store menu opens showing all allowed items, your inventory count, and the current store stock. Use Deposit to move items from your inventory to the store, or Withdraw to take items out. Rank 3+ employees can also adjust item prices from this menu.

Customer Shop

Any player can walk to Config.Locations.customerStore to open the customer shop. Items are browsable by category (Bud, Joints, Misc). Purchase removes cash from the player, deposits to the business bank, reduces store stock, and gives the player the item plus a receipt.

Employee Billing

A qb-target zone on the cash register opens a billing UI. The employee picks from nearby players and selects items from their own inventory to bill. The target player receives an incoming transaction popup and can accept (cash or bank) or decline. On acceptance, money is transferred to the business bank and the employee receives their configured cut (Config.BillingEmployeeCut).

8 — Admin Plant Spot Setup

Plant spots are not hard-coded in config. They are registered in-game by a server admin and stored in the database. Spots persist across server restarts and are broadcast to all clients on join.

Admin Commands

# HotBox
/hotboxsetupplants

# White Widow
/wwsetupplants
Requires the command.noclip ace permission, or god / admin QBCore permission level.

Two placement methods

MethodHow to use
Raycast Selector Click Start Selector in the panel. Aim at an existing weed prop in the world — it highlights in gold. Press E to register it. Press Backspace or Enter to finish.
Marker Placement Click Place Marker. A floating green sphere appears. Use WASD to move, R/F to raise/lower, mouse to orbit camera. Press E to add the current position as a spot, Backspace to undo, Enter to confirm.

When you click Save Spots in the panel, all registered spots are sent to the server, saved to the DB, and broadcast to all connected clients immediately — no restart required.

12 — Notes

  • Both resources are independent — running both simultaneously on one server is fully supported.
  • Plant spots are stored in the DB and survive server restarts. After the initial admin setup you never need to reconfigure them unless you move locations.
  • The growth timer runs server-side. Plants that are planted before a restart will resume from their saved planted_at timestamp and become harvestable on the next growth check (every 10 seconds).
  • The billing employee cut is applied at transaction acceptance time — the employee receives cash, not bank funds.
  • Receipt items are unique: true — a player can only hold one receipt at a time. Use the item to view the receipt in a NUI overlay, then it is consumed.
  • Mugshot uploads require a valid Config.FiveManageApiKey. Without it, photos will fail silently and employee cards will show no photo.
  • Store prices changed in-game are persisted to the DB and survive restarts.
